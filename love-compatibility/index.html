What is a love compatibility calculator?

A love compatibility calculator is a playful online tool that turns a few relationship preferences into a fun match score. It is designed for entertainment and conversation, not scientific prediction.

What can compatibility include?

Real compatibility can involve communication, trust, shared values, lifestyle preferences and how partners handle differences.

Is the score scientifically accurate?

No. The result is for entertainment only and cannot predict whether a relationship will succeed.
