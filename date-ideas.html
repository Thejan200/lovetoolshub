Golden hour

Before you plan

Three things worth remembering

  1. Make it shared. Offer two options instead of making every decision alone.
  2. Choose comfort over spectacle. A quiet activity can be more romantic than a complicated plan.
  3. Leave room to adapt. The best date can change course when both people are having fun.
