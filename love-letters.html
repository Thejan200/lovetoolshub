Love letters & messages

Say it in a way
that feels yours.

A good message does not need grand language. It needs one honest detail and a little care.

Writing menu

A sweet noteA repair messageA short caption

Sweet note prompt

“I noticed something small…”

Start with a detail they might not realise you remember. Then tell them what it means to you.

“I keep thinking about the way you [specific thing]. It makes ordinary moments feel a little lighter. I just wanted you to know I notice.”

Repair message

“I understand why that hurt.”

Acknowledge the impact, take responsibility, and do not ask them to make you feel better.

“I have been thinking about [what happened]. I understand that it may have made you feel [impact]. I am sorry. I am working on [specific change], and I will respect whatever space you need.”
