Love Test

Answer eight questions about affection, communication and connection to generate a fun love score.

What is a love test?

A love test is a lighthearted reflection quiz about relationship habits such as affection, communication, trust, appreciation and shared enjoyment.

What does the score mean?

The score summarizes your answers for entertainment. A higher score means your answers contained more positive responses to the themes in this particular quiz.

Is a love test accurate?

No. This is not a scientific measurement of love and cannot predict relationship outcomes. Use it as a conversation starter rather than a definitive assessment.
