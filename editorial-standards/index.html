OUR STANDARDS

Editorial Standards

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Purpose

LoveToolsHub publishes browser-based love, relationship, and romantic entertainment tools. Our purpose is to offer a positive, clear, and easy-to-use experience for visitors seeking lighthearted reflection or conversation ideas.

Originality and accuracy

We aim to create original page copy, tool prompts, calculations, and results. Where a tool gives a playful score or quiz outcome, we label it as entertainment and avoid presenting it as a factual prediction or scientific conclusion.

Respectful content

We do not encourage manipulation, harassment, or disrespectful treatment of other people. Content should support consent, privacy, healthy boundaries, and clear communication.

Review and updates

We periodically review pages for broken links, unclear language, technical problems, and policy relevance. We may update pages when a tool, disclosure, or standard needs improvement.

Feedback and corrections

To report an error, broken link, or content concern, email thejanmahakumbura@gmail.com. We review feedback and make corrections where appropriate.
