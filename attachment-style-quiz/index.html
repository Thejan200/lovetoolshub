What are attachment styles?

Attachment theory describes patterns in how people experience closeness and security in relationships. Common labels include secure, anxious, avoidant and fearful-avoidant.

Important

This short quiz is only a reflection exercise. It cannot diagnose an attachment disorder or determine your relationship future. People can also show different patterns in different relationships.
